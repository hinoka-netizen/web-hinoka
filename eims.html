Hinoka Enterprise Management System (HEMS) — Konstitusi Operasional PT. Hinoka Alsindo Tekni

Volume 1: Legal & Corporate Foundation

Akta Pendirian, NIB, NPWP, Anggaran Dasar, Corporate Identity, Visi, Misi, & Core Values.

Volume 2: Corporate Governance

Struktur Organisasi, Job Description, Wewenang, Delegasi, Kode Etik, & Budaya Kerja.

Volume 3: Corporate Strategy

Corporate Blueprint, Business Plan, Strategic Planning, SWOT, Balanced Scorecard, & Roadmap.

Volume 4: Corporate Policy

Seluruh Kebijakan Mutu, SDM, Produksi, Keuangan, Purchasing, K3, dan IT.

Volume 5: Quality Management System

Manual Mutu Berstandar ISO 9001:2015 (Klausul 4 hingga 10).

Volume 6: Business Process

Alur Proses Bisnis dari Customer Inquiry, Produksi, QC, Delivery, hingga After Sales.

Volume 7: SOP Master

Seluruh Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) departemen Marketing, Produksi, HRGA, dll.

Volume 8: Prosedur Kerja (PK)

Prosedur teknis pengelasan, pembubutan, pengecatan, assembly, dan rekrutmen.

Volume 9: Instruksi Kerja (IK)

Petunjuk teknis setting mesin, kalibrasi, pengoperasian forklift, dan alat ukur.

Volume 10: Standar Teknis

Drawing, BOM, Parameter Mesin, Spesifikasi Material, dan Standar Dimensi.

Volume 11: Formulir Korporat

Seluruh formulir resmi perusahaan dari FRM-001 hingga Review Manajemen.

Volume 12: Database Perusahaan

Database Supplier, Customer, Mesin, Karyawan, Risk, dan Kalibrasi Aset.

Volume 13: KPI Management

Corporate KPI, Department KPI, Individual KPI, dan Scorecard Penilaian Kinerja.

Volume 14: Risk Management

Risk Register, Risk Matrix, Mitigasi, Business Continuity, & Emergency Response.

Volume 15: Continual Improvement

PDCA, Kaizen, 5S/5R, Lesson Learned, CAPA, dan Program Inovasi.
